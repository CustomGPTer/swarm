Search sources and the DHT index

Swarm comes with two search sources, the Internet Archive and Linux Tracker, which list work that is free to share. They are run by other people, and Swarm is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to either. When you use a source through Swarm, that source's own terms apply to you.

Any other source is one you add yourself, and choosing it is your decision. The developer does not supply, check or recommend other sources.

The DHT index is a list of what your own phone has heard about on a public network. Nobody has checked it. It can include torrents that are not free to share, and things you would rather not see, although titles that look adult are left out unless you turn that off. A torrent appearing in the index, or in a search result, does not mean you have the right to download it.

The BitTorrent network

Swarm depends on trackers, the DHT and other people's devices, all of which are outside the developer's control. A torrent with nobody sharing it will not download, however long you wait. Other people on the network can see your internet address, as the privacy policy explains.

Using BitTorrent can break the rules of some mobile, workplace and public networks. Keeping to the terms of the connection you use is up to you.
