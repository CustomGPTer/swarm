# Swarm

Swarm is a free torrent app for Android that finds torrents too. Search by keyword across open catalogues and sources you add, or search an index of the DHT that the app builds on your phone. Every result shows its seeders, leechers, size and age, and one tap starts the download. It has no adverts, no accounts, no tracking and no paid version.
