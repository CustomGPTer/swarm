Adds a Help page inside the app.

- Help is at the top of Settings and in the side menu. It explains every tab and every setting in plain words, and you can search it
- Step-by-step guides for getting a torrent into Swarm, adding a website as a search source, and setting up Jackett or Prowlarr with a Torznab address, with example addresses
- A plain account of what the DHT is and what the crawler does, and a troubleshooting section for the usual problems
- Shortcuts to the right part of Help from Search sources and the DHT tab

Android 10 or later, 64-bit. Install `Swarm.apk` below, over the version you have. Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/swarm/releases.html
