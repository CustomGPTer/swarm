A free, open-source torrent app

LibreTorrent is free and open source, as Swarm is, and is built on the same torrent engine. Where the two differ is in how you come by a torrent, and in a few things each has that the other does not.

 SwarmLibreTorrent
Finding a torrentSwarmSearch inside the app, across open catalogues and sources you add, or search its own DHT index. Or bring a magnet link, a torrent file or a QR codeLibreTorrentBring a magnet link or a torrent file. Its description lists no search
PlayingSwarmBuilt-in video and music players, and playing a video while it downloadsLibreTorrentStreams files as they download
Feeds and schedulingSwarmRSS feeds with automatic downloading, a watchlist for saved searches, and scheduled hoursLibreTorrentAn Atom and RSS manager with automatic downloading, and scheduling
Where it does moreSwarmSearch, the DHT index, safety warnings on results, and a VPN warningLibreTorrentAndroid TV, a proxy for trackers and peers, moving files while they download, a black theme, and more than 35 languages. Swarm is in English only
Price and licenceSwarmFree and open source, under the GNU General Public License, version 3LibreTorrentFree and open source, under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later
