Version 1.0

First release

LatestReleased 17 November 2026

Added

  • The Torrents tab, with All, Downloading and Complete lists, pause and resume for one torrent or all of them, a filter, and selection for sharing or deleting several at once.
  • Adding a torrent by magnet link, info-hash, the address of a torrent file, or a QR code. Magnet links and torrent files opened in other apps come to Swarm.
  • The Search tab: keyword search across your sources, with seeders, leechers, size and age on every result, sorting, and a filter for video, audio, books and software.
  • Two built-in sources, the Internet Archive and Linux Tracker, and your own: a Torznab address from Jackett or Prowlarr, or the address of a website's results page.
  • The DHT tab: a crawler that builds a searchable index of torrents on your phone, with a choice of when it runs and how large the index may grow.
  • A result page with the file list, the source, safety warnings, a copy button, sharing and a QR code.
  • A page for each torrent with its progress, speed and pieces, and tabs for files, peers and trackers. Untick files, put one first, set speed limits, re-check the files, or move it up and down the queue.
  • Play now, which downloads a video in order so that it can be watched before it has finished.
  • Video and Music tabs with built-in players. Music carries on in the background.
  • Wi-Fi only, download and upload limits, a limit on active torrents, scheduled hours, only while charging, a battery saver and Auto-Shutdown.
  • Seeding to a ratio, not at all, or until you pause it, with uploading kept to Wi-Fi if you choose.
  • A warning when no VPN is on, with the option to stop all torrent traffic without one. Encryption, a blocklist of address ranges, and a filter that leaves adult titles out.
  • Finished downloads sorted into Movies, TV, Music, Books and Other.
  • RSS feeds with filters and automatic downloading, and a watchlist that checks saved searches about once an hour.
  • Making a torrent from a file or folder of your own.
  • A browser that picks up magnet links and torrent files from the pages you open.
  • A backup file holding your torrent list, sources, feeds, watchlist and settings.
  • Light and dark themes, or follow the phone.
  • A notice on the Torrents tab when a newer version is available.

Installing and updating

  • When you download, Chrome may warn that the file might be harmful. It shows this warning for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway.
  • Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.
  • Google Play Protect may say it does not recognise the app, or block it, because Swarm does not come from Google Play. Choose More details, then Install anyway.
  • To update, download the latest version and install it over the one you have. Your torrents, index, sources and settings are kept.
  • Every version is signed with the same key, so Android accepts an update only if it comes from the same developer.
