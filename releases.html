Release notes

What changed in each version of Swarm. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.3 17 November 2026Latest

  • Play now, fixed. Play now could leave the player spinning without ever starting, even with a fifth of the film downloaded. The parts the player needed were being asked for in a way that could make a peer stop answering for a minute at a time. They are now put at the front of the ordinary queue, and the start and the end of the file are asked for the moment you tap Play.
  • Tapping Play several times opened a player for every tap. One tap now opens one player, and further taps are ignored while it is opening or on screen.
  • The Play button showed no sign of having been pressed. It now changes to Opening straight away.
  • The player could take several seconds to appear. It now appears at once, and the slower work happens behind it.
  • While the player is waiting, a line under the spinner says what for: the start of the video, the index at the end of the file, or the next part, with how many people it is connected to and the speed.
  • The player keeps asking for the parts just ahead of where you are watching, so it stops to wait less often.
  • The DHT crawler now slows right down while anything is downloading and pauses while a video is playing, so it no longer competes with them.

Version 1.2 17 November 2026

  • Help, built in. A Help page, at the top of Settings and in the side menu. It explains every tab and every setting in plain words, and you can search it.
  • Step-by-step guides in Help for getting a torrent into Swarm, adding a website as a search source, and setting up Jackett or Prowlarr with a Torznab address, with example addresses to copy.
  • A plain account in Help of what the DHT is, what the crawler does, and what to expect from it.
  • A troubleshooting section for the usual problems: a torrent stuck at the start, slow downloads, downloads stopping with the screen off, and files that cannot be found.
  • Shortcuts to the right part of Help from Search sources and from the DHT tab.
  • Adding a source now starts on Website, the simpler of the two kinds.

Version 1.1 17 November 2026

  • A crawler that keeps going. The DHT crawler could stop collecting once Swarm had been sent to the background and brought back, while still looking switched on. The index then barely grew.
  • The Newest, Seeders and Biggest buttons on the DHT tab did nothing unless you had typed a search.
  • After a change of sort order, the Search and DHT lists stayed on the row you were looking at, so the new order was out of sight. They now go back to the top.
  • Results from the Internet Archive showed no seeders and a red dot. They are web seeded, and now say so. A tracker that has never heard of a torrent no longer counts as nobody sharing it.
  • The crawler fetches the details of four times as many torrents at once, and asks more of the network for torrents to look at.
  • Torrents in the DHT index show the seeders and peers seen when they were found.
  • Website sources that list results without download links now work. Swarm shows the list, and gets the link from the result's own page when you tap Download. If that page only shows its link after a tap, Swarm opens it in its browser and picks the link up when you tap it.
  • Websites that build their results with scripts, or check for a browser first, are read the way a browser reads them.
  • The Test button for a new source says when a site keeps its download links on each result's own page.

Version 1.0 17 November 2026

  • The Torrents tab, with All, Downloading and Complete lists, pause and resume for one torrent or all of them, a filter, and selection for sharing or deleting several at once.
  • Adding a torrent by magnet link, info-hash, the address of a torrent file, or a QR code. Magnet links and torrent files opened in other apps come to Swarm.
  • The Search tab: keyword search across your sources, with seeders, leechers, size and age on every result, sorting, and a filter for video, audio, books and software.
  • Two built-in sources, the Internet Archive and Linux Tracker, and your own: a Torznab address from Jackett or Prowlarr, or the address of a website's results page.
  • The DHT tab: a crawler that builds a searchable index of torrents on your phone, with a choice of when it runs and how large the index may grow.
  • A result page with the file list, the source, safety warnings, a copy button, sharing and a QR code.
  • A page for each torrent with its progress, speed and pieces, and tabs for files, peers and trackers. Untick files, put one first, set speed limits, re-check the files, or move it up and down the queue.
  • Play now, which downloads a video in order so that it can be watched before it has finished.
  • Video and Music tabs with built-in players. Music carries on in the background.
  • Wi-Fi only, download and upload limits, a limit on active torrents, scheduled hours, only while charging, a battery saver and Auto-Shutdown.
  • Seeding to a ratio, not at all, or until you pause it, with uploading kept to Wi-Fi if you choose.
  • A warning when no VPN is on, with the option to stop all torrent traffic without one. Encryption, a blocklist of address ranges, and a filter that leaves adult titles out.
  • Finished downloads sorted into Movies, TV, Music, Books and Other.
  • RSS feeds with filters and automatic downloading, and a watchlist that checks saved searches about once an hour.
  • Making a torrent from a file or folder of your own.
  • A browser that picks up magnet links and torrent files from the pages you open.
  • A backup file holding your torrent list, sources, feeds, watchlist and settings.
  • Light and dark themes, or follow the phone.
  • A notice on the Torrents tab when a newer version is available.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.3 is called Swarm.apk. It is 61.1 MB (61,143,520 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

9bc02494c6ed06c1d6bdd882d14b654d5822700774715f1fce7eb871e2e952ae

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Swarm.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
